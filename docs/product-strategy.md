# VIRALY AI Product Strategy

## Positioning

VIRALY AI is a mobile growth cockpit for TikTok creators. The product should feel like a coach that converts noisy signals into practical publishing decisions.

## Differentiation

Many creator analytics products focus on reporting. VIRALY AI should focus on action:

- translate account metrics into daily next moves;
- combine account analytics, video review, idea scoring, niche strategy, and revenue paths;
- make advice measurable with confidence scores;
- explain why each recommendation matters;
- help creators build a repeatable content cycle instead of chasing random viral posts.

## Core User Promise

"Connect your TikTok account, pick a video or idea, and know what to post next to grow traffic, revenue, and audience quality."

## MVP Pillars

1. TikTok account analysis
   - account stage, follower trend, top content clusters, posting windows;
   - best next actions for the next 24-48 hours.

2. Video Lab
   - gallery upload;
   - hook, retention, shareability, save intent, CTA, and revenue fit;
   - before-publish checklist.

3. Idea Lab
   - score an idea before filming;
   - turn one idea into hook, structure, CTA, and distribution plan.

4. Niche and Posting Strategy
   - compare niche options by audience pain, saturation, revenue path, and content repeatability;
   - build a weekly posting cycle with testing windows.

5. Creator Coach
   - answer questions around best time to post, stories, engagement habits, posting volume, and niche choice;
   - convert each answer into an action.

## Monetization Paths For Users

- Lead magnets from high-save videos.
- Affiliate recommendations linked to tutorial content.
- Paid audits for audience members.
- Templates or systems sold through profile link.
- Brand deal readiness once the account has repeatable signal.

## North Star

The creator should open VIRALY AI and immediately know the next useful action, not just see yesterday's numbers.
